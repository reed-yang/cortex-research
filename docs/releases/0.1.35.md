# Product 0.1.35

Status: built and installed on the operator host on 2026-10-08 (code-only
upgrade from 0.1.34). None of the 473 Library titles shows as `[redacted]`
afterwards, and the blog whose title did now shows its title and every line.

Text of an external source (a paper, a blog, an XHS note and the
recommendations it carries) is no longer redacted for the reasoning markers
`<thinking>`, "hidden reasoning", "internal reasoning" and "chain of thought".
Those markers keep a model's own reasoning out of what Cortex produces; papers
and blogs discuss chain-of-thought as a subject, and on the operator host the
rule matched 218 lines across the Library and replaced the title of one blog. Source
titles in the Library, a note's links and a research run's source list follow
the same rule. Private paths and credentials stay redacted everywhere. Events,
research items and research documents keep every pattern.

The Control schema stays 21 and the worker `hermes-0.15.0-gen10` is unchanged,
so an upgrade from 0.1.34 is code-only.
