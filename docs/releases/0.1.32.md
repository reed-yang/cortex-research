# Product 0.1.32

Status: built and installed on the operator host on 2026-10-07 (code-only
upgrade from 0.1.31). The page-1 XHS smoke then imported 73 blogs with it.

An imported blog now keeps copies of its article's images. The blog fetch
extracts the article with its images and copies each PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP
under the same public-address policy as the page (at most 10 MiB each, 60
images and 60 MiB per article) into the blog version's `assets/`, so the
Library reader shows them. Any other image keeps its URL and the reader names
its host. The Control schema stays 21 and the worker `hermes-0.15.0-gen10` is
unchanged, so an upgrade from 0.1.31 is code-only.
